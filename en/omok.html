Harang K-Omok (Gomoku)

Free · No ads

Omok is the Korean name for Gomoku — five in a row on a Go board. Play the computer or a friend right in your Chrome side panel.

Add to Chrome — Free

How to play

  • Black and White take turns placing a stone on an empty intersection. Black goes first.
  • The first to line up five stones in a row — across, down or diagonally — wins.
  • K-Omok uses free-style rules: no forbidden moves (no 3-3, 4-4 or overline bans). Six or more in a row also wins.

Tips

  • Block an open three (three stones with both ends empty) right away, or it becomes an open four you can’t stop.
  • Look for a move that makes a three and a four at the same time — that usually wins.
  • Start near the center and build diagonals to open more lines of attack.
