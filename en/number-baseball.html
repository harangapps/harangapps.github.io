Harang Number Baseball

Free · No ads

Number Baseball is a classic Korean code-breaking game, similar to Bulls and Cows or Mastermind. Keep it open in your Chrome side panel for a quick brain break.

Add to Chrome — Free

How to play

  • The computer picks a secret number made of 3 (or 4) different digits. It can include 0.
  • Enter a guess and you get a hint. A Strike (S) is the right digit in the right place. A Ball (B) is the right digit in the wrong place.
  • Example: if the answer is 352 and you guess 325, the 3 is a strike and the 2 and 5 are balls → 1S 2B.
  • Get all strikes (3S or 4S) to win. The fewer tries, the better your record.

Tips

  • Start with guesses that don't share digits, like 123 and 456, to find which digits are in the answer.
  • A guess with no hits ("Out") is a great clue: you can cross out all of those digits.
  • Once you know the digits, swap their positions to collect strikes. A 3-digit game usually takes 5–7 tries.

Features

  • 3-digit and 4-digit modes
  • Every guess is listed, so it's easy to reason
  • Best record saved for each mode
  • Keyboard input (Enter to check, Backspace to delete)
  • Side panel or popup window
  • 9 languages
  • No account, no ads, no tracking — completely free

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Every feature is free and there are no ads.

Which browser does it work in?

It's a Chrome extension from the Chrome Web Store. It opens in the side panel on Chrome 116 or later.

Does it collect my data?

No. Your settings and records stay on your own computer.
