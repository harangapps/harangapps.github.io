개인정보처리방침

시행일: 2026년 11월 14일 · 대상: 하랑(Harang) 무료 크롬 확장프로그램

1. 한눈에 보기

  • 하랑 확장프로그램은 이름, 이메일, 연락처 같은 개인정보를 수집하지 않아요.
  • 게임 기록·설정 등은 사용자 기기(브라우저) 안에만 저장되고, 개발자 서버로 보내지 않아요.
  • 광고, 분석 도구, 추적 코드를 쓰지 않으며 제3자에게 어떤 정보도 팔거나 공유하지 않아요.

2. 기기 안에 저장하는 정보

확장프로그램이 동작하는 데 필요한 정보만 브라우저 저장소(chrome.storage, localStorage)에 보관해요.

  • 언어·화면 설정 (사이드패널/팝업 등)
  • 게임 기록, 최고 기록, 진행 중인 게임 상태
  • "하랑 소식"을 읽었는지 여부

이 정보는 확장프로그램을 삭제하면 함께 지워져요.

3. 📰 하랑 소식

새 기능·새 제품 소식을 보여 주기 위해 확장프로그램이 가끔 https://harangapps.github.io/news.json 파일을 읽어 와요. 이때 사용자를 구별하는 정보(아이디, 기록 등)는 보내지 않아요. 이 파일은 GitHub Pages에서 제공되며, GitHub는 일반적인 웹 서버처럼 접속 기록(IP 주소 등)을 남길 수 있어요. 자세한 내용은 GitHub 개인정보처리방침을 참고해 주세요.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026 · Applies to: Harang free Chrome extensions

1. Summary

  • Harang extensions do not collect personal information such as your name, email or contacts.
  • Game records and settings are stored only on your device (in your browser) and are never sent to our servers.
  • We use no ads, analytics or tracking code, and we never sell or share any data with third parties.

2. Data stored on your device

Only what the extension needs to work is kept in browser storage (chrome.storage, localStorage):

  • Language and display settings (side panel / popup, etc.)
  • Game records, best scores and games in progress
  • Whether you have read "Harang News"

This data is removed when you uninstall the extension.

3. 📰 Harang News

To show news about new features and products, the extension occasionally reads the file https://harangapps.github.io/news.json. No information that identifies you (IDs, records, etc.) is sent. The file is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, may log access data such as IP addresses. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

4. Product details

⚾ Harang Number Baseball

Stores game records, best scores and settings on your device only.

🧩 Harang Sliding Puzzle

Stores game records and settings on your device only.

🔊 Harang Volume Booster

To boost the volume of a tab you choose, the extension processes that tab's audio in real time, on your device only (tabCapture and offscreen permissions). Audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. To list tabs that are playing sound, it reads tab titles and icons (tabs permission) but never stores or sends them. Per-tab volume is kept only while the browser is running; only your language setting is stored on your device.

🔢 Harang Sudoku

Stores game records, progress and settings on your device only.

🧮 Harang AllCalc

All calculations and unit conversions run on your device. Only language and display settings are stored on your device.

⚫ Harang Reversi

Stores game records and settings on your device only.

⚪ Harang K-Omok

Stores game records and settings on your device only.

🎋 Harang K-Yut

Stores game records, the game in progress and settings (such as your team) on your device only. The recipe link on the result screen opens the Harang website in a new tab.

5. Children's privacy

Harang extensions do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in "Harang News".
